Lassonde Curve Explorer

The Lassonde curve is the valuation index of a lunar in-situ resource programme against years from 1 January 2030. The index rises through Exploration and Discovery, falls through Feasibility, rises from the trough through Construction and Rampup to 1.00 at the target time, and then follows programme value through Reserve Depletion (Equations 53 to 65). The template is the index shape before the target time, the standard curve is the same shape at target time 12.5 yr with a fixed yearly fall after it, and tT is the target time.
